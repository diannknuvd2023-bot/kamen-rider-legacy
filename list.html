Heisei Phase 2 Kamen Rider (2009–2018)

Heisei Phase 2 marks a mature and innovative era of the Kamen Rider series, spanning from Kamen Rider W to Kamen Rider Zi-O.

This period emphasizes multiple Riders, partnerships, and shared worlds, expanding beyond the concept of a lone hero.

The series explores modern themes such as memory, desire, technology, and time,

while introducing collectible transformation systems that became a defining visual feature.

Concluding with Zi-O, a tribute to the entire Heisei era, Phase 2 stands as a major milestone in the franchise’s evolution.

Kamen Rider W (2009)

A dual-hero detective story set in a city driven by memories.

Its core theme is partnership and shared identity,

with stylish noir elements and a strong sense of justice rooted in human bonds.

Kamen Rider OOO (2010)

A story centered on desire and temptation, where power comes at a cost.

The series explores human greed, selflessness,

and what truly defines fulfillment through constantly changing forms.

Kamen Rider Fourze (2011)

A bright and optimistic series themed around friendship and youth.

Set in a high school, it emphasizes unity, trust, and reaching out to others—

even enemies—through sheer positivity.

Kamen Rider Wizard (2012)

A battle between hope and despair,

focusing on protecting the hearts of others.

Magic-based action and a darker tone highlight emotional resilience

in the face of loss and suffering.

Kamen Rider Gaim (2013)

A dramatic tale of choice, power, and sacrifice set against a world-changing conflict.

What begins as a dance-themed rivalry evolves into

a mythic struggle over fate and survival.

Kamen Rider Drive (2014)

A fusion of police drama and superhero action,

themed around stopping time and crime.

The story examines justice, responsibility,

and coexistence between humans and artificial beings.

Kamen Rider Ghost (2015)

A life-and-death journey centered on legacy and purpose.

By confronting historical heroes and philosophical ideals,

the series questions what it truly means to live meaningfully.

Kamen Rider EX-AID (2016)

A high-energy blend of medical ethics and gaming culture.

Behind its colorful visuals lies a serious exploration of life, death,

and the responsibility of saving others.

Kamen Rider Build (2017)

A complex narrative about war, identity, and redemption.

Through science and fractured memories,

the series explores the consequences of conflict and the search for truth.

Kamen Rider ZI-O (2018)

A time-traveling anniversary work reflecting on destiny and legacy.

Serving as a tribute to the entire Heisei era,

it questions whether one can escape fate—or choose a better future.
